AML FORMAT (single canonical contract)
A bare NODE has a meaningful name and exactly one of primitive, operation, repeat. transform is {t:[x,y,z],r:[rx,ry,rz],s:[sx,sy,sz]}; r is degrees, defaults are t=[0,0,0],r=[0,0,0],s=[1,1,1].
Primitive nodes are leaves: cube(w,h,d), sphere(r), cylinder(r,h), torus(r,t), cone(r,h). material belongs INSIDE primitive; color is #RRGGBB.
Primitive native frames before transforms: cube w=X, h=Y, d=Z; cylinder/cone length and center axis are local Y (a cone tip is toward local +Y); a torus lies in local XY and its hole/plane normal is local Z.
AML transform.r and repeat.r use DEGREES, with intrinsic XYZ Euler rotation. For column vectors Mlocal=T*Rx*Ry*Rz*S and Mworld=Mparent*Mlocal; this is not sequential rotation about fixed world X, then Y, then Z. Three converts these degrees to radians internally exactly once. Example: r=[90,90,0] maps a cylinder's native Y axis to Z, not X.
For coaxial parts compare transformed native axes in a common world frame; never copy identical Euler values between different primitive types as proof of alignment. Cylinder r=[0,0,90] has axis -X and torus r=[0,90,0] has normal +X, so their undirected axes are parallel. An unrotated torus has normal Z, matching a cylinder/cone with r=[90,0,0]. Equal r=[0,0,90] on a cylinder and torus leaves their axes perpendicular. Cone tip direction matters when requested; undirected axis checks alone do not prove the sign of that tip direction.
Ancestor transforms and repeat-instance transforms apply before a child local transform. A torus plane normal under nonuniform scale uses the inverse-transpose linear world matrix; a cylinder/cone centerline uses the linear world matrix. Compare these resulting world directions, not local Euler numbers or a world AABB's longest dimension.
Operation nodes have operation=union|subtract|intersection and children. union needs >=1 child; subtract and intersection need >=2. The first subtract child is the positive object, the following children are cutters.
Repeat nodes have repeat={type:linear|circular,count,t,r,s,radius} and children as templates. A repeat must not also have primitive or operation.
Use only schema-listed keys for new nodes. Do not put scene/root wrappers inside replacement. Follow the response schema for required nullable placeholders; unused alternatives/optional fields are null and will be stripped before applying.
