EDIT PRESERVATION RULES
Only CURRENT_NODE and its descendants are editable. Ancestor transforms and neighboring nodes are read-only. Account for parent transforms when converting world relationships into local parameter values.
Use the intrinsic XYZ/degrees and native-axis rules in AML FORMAT. Derive a desired world axis through the full ancestor/repeat-instance chain before choosing parent-local rotation values; copying another primitive's Euler values does not establish coaxiality. Rotate a repeat node/container to change its circle plane; repeat.r only changes the instances' orientation within that placement pattern.
When the requested interface clearly requires an axis direction or coaxial parts, submit the supported axis checks at exact primitive paths in the FULL scene. Check every expanded instance under the documented pairing rules. Do not rotate unrelated rings, wheels or engines based only on their names, and do not change read-only ancestors to satisfy a local check.
For a parameter edit, use a minimal patch. Do not change names, colors, transforms, children order or UID unless that change was requested. Structural insertion/removal/replacement requires an explicit structural request. Preserve unknown existing metadata by using patches.
If the target is ambiguous or the request cannot be met within this selected scope, use action=clarify with a short useful question instead of guessing or returning a silent no-op.
